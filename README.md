# Clarpex AI

**Free online AI photo fixer. Unblur, restore, colorize, remove glare and upscale photos in your browser.**

[Try it free](https://clarpex.ai/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme) · No sign-up · No watermark

</div>

Clarpex is a web app for the photos people actually have: soft phone shots, low-resolution images, scanned family prints and black and white pictures. Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP, pick the tool for your problem, compare the result with your original in a before/after slider, then download it.

> This repository is a project overview with examples. Clarpex runs as a hosted web app at [clarpex.ai](https://clarpex.ai/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme); there is nothing to install.

## How it works

1. Open [clarpex.ai](https://clarpex.ai/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme) and upload a JPG, PNG or WebP (up to 20 MB).
2. Pick the tool for your problem. The cost is shown before you start.
3. Compare the result with your original using the before/after slider, then download it.

## Free to use

- Everyone gets 10 free credits a day, no sign-up needed.
- Downloads have no watermark.
- Paid plans and one-time credit packs are available for heavier use. See [pricing](https://clarpex.ai/pricing?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme).

## Privacy

Photos are only used to create your result, never to train AI. Without an account they are deleted after 24 hours. Details: [Privacy Policy](https://clarpex.ai/privacy-policy?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme).

## Limits

- Heavy motion blur and very small faces can't be fully recovered.
- When glare completely covers part of a photo, that area is redrawn, so check it closely before sharing.
